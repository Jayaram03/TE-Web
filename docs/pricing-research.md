## Publishing a numeric price

Obtain an approved supplier quote for specified travel dates, group size, room occupancy, named hotel/category, daily meal plan, transport type, activities, permits, taxes, flight/visa treatment and cancellation terms. Specify whether the displayed price is per person or per group and its validity period. Confirm inclusions separately from the illustrative itinerary before booking. Until then, enquiry is the source of a personalised quote.
